The Montessori Method

Why we teach this way

A short introduction to the four ideas behind everything that happens in our classrooms.

Sensitive Periods

Children pass through natural windows where they're especially drawn to certain skills — we meet them there.

Prepared Environment

Every shelf and material has a purpose, sized and ordered for a child to use independently.

Freedom Within Limits

Children choose their own work within a structure that keeps everyone safe and respectful.

The Trained Guide

Teachers observe closely and step in only to guide, never to interrupt a child's concentration.
