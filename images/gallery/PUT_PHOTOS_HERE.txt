Save real school photos and videos into this folder
(e.g. sports-day-2026.jpg, graduation-2026.mp4).

Then list each one in js/gallery-data.js so it shows up on the
Gallery page. Open gallery.html and see the "Add Photos & Videos"
box near the top for the exact code format to copy — it covers
photos, local video files, and embedded videos (e.g. YouTube).
