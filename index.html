Our Approach

A prepared place for every child to grow

At Peculia Gift Montessori School, children are trusted to lead their own learning. Every classroom is arranged as a "prepared environment" — real, child-sized materials that let each child practice, repeat, and master a skill at their own pace, guided (not directed) by trained teachers.

Child-led learning

Children choose purposeful activities that match their stage of development.

Hands-on materials

Real, tactile Montessori materials build concentration and independence.

Mixed-age classrooms

Older children mentor younger ones, building confidence on both sides.
